# Six Japanese dialogue-trial stories — draft for review

These are newly written candidate materials, not stories extracted from the research-plan document. They were drafted in this chat, not generated by a locally running Nemotron model. They are not validated or frozen for participant use.

The supplied plan requires six dialogue stories, three additional reference stories, and one unscored practice story. This file covers the six dialogue stories only. No story is permanently assigned to a silence threshold: allocate stories and condition order across participants in advance to reduce story/condition confounding.

## Proposed scoring convention for review

Each story has seven candidate information units (0/1 each; total 0–7). Accept an unambiguous Japanese paraphrase, not only the exact wording. Count each unit once across the full recording, including recall after the encouragement. A bare mention of an item without its relevant story relationship is insufficient. When a unit includes an attribute (for example, a red envelope), both the object and attribute are required under this draft rule. Names and unlisted connective details receive no additional points. Finalize accepted variants, contradictions, corrections, and ambiguous references before collection and use the same rubric for all participants.

## Before freezing materials

Have Japanese-speaking researchers review naturalness, familiarity, and scoring ambiguity. Similar sentence structure does not establish equivalent recall difficulty. These drafts deliberately share a structure, which can introduce practice effects and interference between stories; review whether greater narrative variety is needed before freezing. Check spoken length, intelligibility, pronunciation, and audio duration using the selected voice. Seven scored units do not mean seven equally difficult units.

Generate and save audio before sessions. Freeze text, rubric, audio, voice/speed settings, and allocation table together with version identifiers. Use the same story recording wherever that story is assigned. Do not regenerate stories or encouragement dynamically during a participant trial. Keep the observer's scoring material separate from participant screens.

The planned reference stories and practice story are still required. The source plan contains no actual story scripts to reuse.
